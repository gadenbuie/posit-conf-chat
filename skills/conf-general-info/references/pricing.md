# posit::conf(2026) Pricing

## In-person passes

In-person passes include full access to the virtual experience. All-access includes workshops (Sept 14, first-come first-serve) plus two conference days (Sept 15–16): all keynotes, 100+ talks, evening events, The Lounge and demos, session live-stream and on-demand talks, Discord server, and sponsors. Conference-only and workshops-only passes are also available.

| Tier | Deadline | All Access | Conference only | Workshops only |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Super Early Bird | Ends April 10, 2026 | $1,998 | $1,099 | $899 |
| Early Bird | Ends June 22, 2026 | $2,098 | $1,199 | $899 |
| Academic (students & educators) | | $1,298 | $749 | $549 |
| General | From June 23, 2026 | $2,248 | $1,349 | $899 |
