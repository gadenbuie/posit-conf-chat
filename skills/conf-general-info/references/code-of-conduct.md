# posit::conf Code of Conduct

Posit Software, PBC — September 28, 2023. Full text: https://posit.co/code-of-conduct/

Everyone who comes to learn and enjoy the experience should feel welcome at posit::conf. Posit is committed to providing a professional, friendly and safe environment for all participants at its events, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, disability, race, ethnicity, religion, national origin or other protected class. This code of conduct applies to all participants: attendees, sponsors, speakers, vendors, media, exhibitors, and volunteers. Posit will actively enforce it throughout posit::conf.

## Inclusiveness

Posit users represent many points on the spectrums of race, color, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sexual orientation, gender, gender identity, age, abilities, wealth, language, and politics. All who come to learn and enjoy the experience are welcome without prejudice; attendees are asked to help make posit::conf a fantastic time for everyone.

## Diversity

Posit celebrates the diversity of the community by reducing or removing barriers to conference attendance and participation, and considers diversity as part of the selection criteria for speakers, organizers, and workshop instructors.

## Accessibility

The conference space provides:

- Gender neutral bathrooms
- A lactation room
- A quiet room
- Accessible options for attendee transportation, hotel rooms, and conference events
- A live streaming option for the general conference sessions

## Anti-harassment

Everyone at posit::conf, in-person or virtual, is expected to contribute to a welcoming, safe, and tolerant environment. Harassment of participants in any form is not tolerated, including:

- Harassing, intimidating, or discriminatory verbal, physical, or visual conduct; posting or wearing inappropriate, graphic, or profane images or slogans
- Verbal abuse of attendees, speakers, staff, or sponsors (including sexually explicit language, gestures, or images on any virtual platform)
- Cloaking your identity by misidentifying yourself or using an offensive name

Organizers may take action against anything designed to disrupt the event or make the environment hostile. Anyone asked to stop harassing behavior must comply immediately. Violations result in removal from the event with no refund. Posit moderators monitor public chat channels and sli.do questions; event managers are on site and will act to keep the event a safe space.

## Reporting

- In person: contact any posit::conf staff member (staff t-shirt) or the conference general information desk
- Email: codeofconduct@posit.co
- Phone: 844-448-1212 (monitored for the duration of the event)

Staff will help you contact hotel/venue security, local law enforcement, or local support, and will handle reports privately and respectfully; you may bring someone to support you. Posit will take appropriate action at its reasonable good faith discretion.

## Badges and credentials

Attendee badges and access credentials are personal and non-transferable; unregistered individuals and minors cannot access the event unless agreed with Posit in advance. Badges display your name, job title, and email address — you are responsible for their security and for any loss or misplacement.

## Data protection

Registration information is collected for registration and payment, credentials, and communication about the conference, shared with Posit staff and instructors for those purposes. See https://posit.co/about/privacy-policy/ — Posit never sells personal information. Sharing data with exhibitors, sponsors, or third parties (including badge scanning) is voluntary and governed by that third party's privacy policy, not Posit's.

## Photos, videos, and content

Attendees may appear in photos/videos taken at the conference, which Posit may use for promotion; participants consent to this by attending. Opt out at registration with a "no-photo" lanyard. Event content is copyrighted; capture, use, or reproduction outside the event without the content owner's permission is prohibited. Speakers retain ownership of their materials and must opt in to publication.

## COVID-19

Posit follows current CDC guidance for attendee health and safety. For accommodations or concerns, contact conf@posit.co.
