# posit::conf(2026) Venue Reference: Venue & Lobby

Venue: Hilton Americas-Houston (1600 Lamar St, Houston, TX 77010)
Dates: September 14–16, 2026 (Workshops: Sept 14 | General Conf: Sept 15–16)

## Airports & Ground Transportation
Airports:
William P. Hobby Airport (HOU): ~10 miles (~20–25 min drive). Closest for domestic flights.
George Bush Intercontinental Airport (IAH): ~21 miles (~30–45 min drive depending on traffic).

Transit Options: Rideshare (Uber/Lyft pickup zones outside baggage claim), taxis, and METRO bus/light rail lines connecting downtown Houston.

## Parking
On-Site Hotel Parking: Hilton Americas-Houston offers valet parking at $65/day. There are no conference parking vouchers.
Public Garages: Several surface lots and garages are located within 1–2 blocks (e.g., Avenida Houston parking garages).

## Badge Pickup & Check-In Logistics
Location: Conference Registration Desk in the main conference foyer (follow Posit signage).
Check-In Requirements: Confirmation email/QR code or photo ID.
Early / Day-Before Pickup: Registration opens the afternoon/evening of Sunday, September 13 (prior to Day 1 workshops) and runs through Wednesday morning.
Lanyards: Specific colored lanyards are available at the desk for attendees who opt out of photography/videography.
Code of Conduct / Help Escalations: All incident reports should be directed to the Posit Registration Desk (not the hotel concierge) or via the dedicated hotline: 844-448-1212.

## Wi-Fi & Power Outlets
Wi-Fi: Free high-speed event Wi-Fi is provided throughout all conference session rooms and public spaces. The network name and password are printed on attendee badges (also on signage and in the mobile event app).
Power: Session tables and breakout rooms have power strips/charging stations; additional power is available in The Lounge.
Demos / Demo Zone: Fourth Floor Foyer.

## Luggage Storage & Coat Check
Hotel Bell Desk: Available 24/7 at the hotel main lobby for standard luggage holding before check-in or after checkout.
Departure Day (Sept 16): A dedicated conference luggage hold area will be available near the registration foyer until talks conclude at ~5:30 PM.

## Restrooms (Including Gender-Neutral)
Standard multi-stall restrooms are located outside each major ballroom and breakout corridor.
Designated All-Gender / Gender-Neutral restrooms: Fourth Floor, one on each side of the ballroom; single-occupancy accessible restrooms are clearly signed in the conference concourse and marked on the event app venue map.

## Quiet Rooms, Work / Call Booths & Dedicated Rooms (Third Floor)
Meditation / Prayer Room: Room 332.
Mother's / Lactation Room: Room 333 — private, locked, with comfortable seating, power outlets, and a refrigerator; key access is managed at the Posit Registration Desk.
Speaker Lounge / Green Room: Room 334.
Opportunity Scholarship Lunch (Monday): Room 338.
Quiet / Sensory Space: a dedicated low-stimulation space for relaxation, meditation, and accessibility needs. Strictly NO phone calls, Zoom meetings, typing with sound, or conversations.
Phone Calls / Remote Work: Direct attendees to The Lounge, the hotel mezzanine, lobby alcoves, or open lounge seating throughout the property.

## Meals & Workshop Day Logistics
Breakfast is not provided; coffee starts at 8:30 am, with AM snacks during the first break on conference days 1 & 2.
Workshop Day (Monday, Sept 14): Full breakfast, morning coffee/tea, lunch, and afternoon refreshments are provided only to registered workshop attendees and instructors.
Main Conf Days (Tuesday Sept 15 – Wednesday Sept 16): Morning coffee/tea, daily lunch, and break refreshments included for all registered conference attendees.
Coffee: Fourth Floor Foyer.
Dietary Restrictions & Allergies: Vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free items are clearly labeled at buffets. Attendees who pre-requested special meals (Kosher, Halal, severe allergies/Celiac) should check in directly with the banquet staff/meal coordinators stationed at the dining entrance; for Kosher or Halal meals on site, find the nearest Hilton employee and they will retrieve the meal from the culinary team.

## Session-Room AV & Speaker Tech
Display Format: 16:9 widescreen standard.
Connectivity: Standard HDMI connections and adapters are provided at speaker podiums.
Room Support: Dedicated AV technicians are assigned to each track room to assist with microphones, stage monitoring, and presentation switching.

## Evening Events & Receptions
Badges Required: Badges must be worn for admission to all social events. Outside guests/plus-ones cannot be accommodated.
Welcome Reception: Monday, September 14 (Evening) — Fourth Floor Foyer + Ballroom, featuring the Hex sticker wall and photo ops (book signings happen in The Lounge; see on-site-extras.md).
Tuesday Night Party (80s Night): Tuesday, September 15 (Evening) — Fourth Floor Foyer + Ballroom.
Birds of a Feather (BoF) sessions: Tuesday and Wednesday during lunch.
