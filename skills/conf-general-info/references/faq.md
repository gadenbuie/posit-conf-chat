# posit::conf(2026) FAQ

## General

- **What is posit::conf()?** Posit's annual conference for R and Python in data science, with workshops, talks, and networking opportunities for the data science community.
- **Where?** Hilton Americas-Houston, 1600 Lamar St, Houston, TX 77010.
- **When and what happens?** September 14–16, 2026 (Monday–Wednesday). Workshops on Sept 14, main conference Sept 15–16. A special R/Pharma pre-conf Summit runs Sunday, Sept 13 (add-on, $499). Conference days feature Birds of a Feather (BoF) sessions, The Lounge, live demos, games and swag, and an evening reception.
- **Who should attend?** Data professionals, with emphasis on data scientists, data science managers, and data decision makers.
- **Is there a virtual option?** Yes. Virtual tickets include the live stream of all keynotes and sessions on Sept 15–16. New for 2026: a Posit-led half-day virtual workshop on Sept 14 (add-on ticket, limited seats, offered at three times), plus an afternoon of virtual-only talks.
- **Mobile app?** The conference mobile app is available; registered attendees received download instructions by email. It has the full agenda, session Q&A, and the venue map.
- **Dress code?** Casual; bring layers.
- **Swag?** Yes.
- **Questions?** Email conf@posit.co.

## Opportunity Scholarships

40 scholarships for members of underrepresented groups (people of color, people with disabilities, elders/older adults, LGBTQ folks, women/gender minorities). Each includes conference registration, a workshop of choice, four hotel nights, and travel reimbursement:

- Domestic: up to $1,000 airfare (economy) + $275 incidentals
- International: up to $2,500 airfare (economy) + $275 incidentals

Details:

- Scholarship applications are handled before the conference; questions to conf@posit.co.
- Visa letters provided if needed.
- Conference, hotel, and workshop are fully covered. Airfare and airport transportation are paid upfront and reimbursed (economy class only).
- Reimbursable: economy airfare, baggage fees, airport↔hotel transportation, and the $275 incidentals allowance (travel-day food, breakfasts and dinners during conf, train/cab, laundry).
- Midday meals are provided free by the conference.
- Receipts must show provider name, date, and amount; airfare receipts also need full name, origin/destination, and travel dates.
- Additional reimbursements only considered if funds remain after standard reimbursements.

## Workshops

- Full list: conf.posit.co/2026/sessions.
- Switching: on site, ask at the registration desk.
- Virtual option: half-day workshop on Sept 14 at three times. In-person workshops are not recorded; workshop materials are posted at https://github.com/posit-conf-2026/ before the workshops begin.
- Discounts: only the academic rate; no other workshop discounts.
- All in-person programs at the Hilton Americas-Houston; room locations are in the mobile app.
- Bring your own laptop.
- Birds of a Feather: networking sessions for attendees with shared interests, held Tuesday and Wednesday during lunch.

## Speakers

- Call for papers: closed (it ran January 15 – February 20).
- Accepted speakers get waived registration.
- Selected speakers may get needs-based travel assistance (flights and accommodations; other needs case-by-case). This is a speaker program only, not an attendee service.

## Registration

- Register at conf.posit.co/2026/registration — one link covers conference, workshop, and R/Pharma Summit add-ons.
- Registration includes: two days of keynotes, talks, The Lounge, exhibitors; morning coffee/tea, break refreshments, and lunch daily. Wear your badge at evening events. No unregistered outside guests.
- Virtual experience requires separate registration (use the Register Now button).

## Cancellations & Substitutions

- Cancel by emailing conf@posit.co.
- Full refund for any reason was available until July 20, 2026; after that, refunds are case-by-case via conf@posit.co.
- Registration transfer to another person: email conf@posit.co.

## Discounts

- **Academic:** students, teachers/faculty, university staff teaching R/Python, and staff maintaining data science teaching servers. Applied automatically during registration. Available until the event start or sellout.
- **Public sector:** government employees (all levels), non-profits, international NGOs. Applied automatically. Not available on Super Early Bird tickets.
- **Groups:** 3+ people from the same organization pay $1,099/person. Email conf@posit.co for a group discount code.

## Conduct, Privacy, Health

- No vaccination or mask requirement currently; policy follows CDC guidance.
- Code of conduct violations: report at the conference registration desk (not the hotel concierge desk) or call 844-448-1212. See [on-site-extras.md](on-site-extras.md) for reporting confidentiality and [code-of-conduct.md](code-of-conduct.md) for the full policy.

## Accessibility

- Assistance (e.g., wheelchair users): contact conf@posit.co in advance or visit the registration desk on site.
- See [venue.md](venue.md) for mother's room, meditation/quiet space, restrooms, and service animals.
