# posit::conf(2026) Quick Reference: Virtual Experience & Platform FAQs

## Platform, Browser Requirements & Login Access
Platform Name: Zuddl (Web-based portal + mobile app).
Portal URL / Login: Access via conf.posit.co/2026 (login link opens closer to event date). Attendees log in using the email address tied to their registration.
Browser Requirements: Modern versions of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Apple Safari (Chrome recommended for optimal video rendering and interactive features). Ensure cookies and JavaScript are enabled.
Firewall / VPN Workarounds: If corporate firewalls or VPNs block Zuddl video streams or WebSockets:
Disconnect from corporate VPN or switch to an open/personal network or hotspot.
Whitelist *.zuddl.com and related media domains with corporate IT.
Switch to mobile view via the official event app on a personal device.


## Livestreams & Schedule Navigation
Watching Talks: Log into the portal and select "Schedule" to browse the agenda and join upcoming sessions, or click "Stages" to jump directly into talks broadcasting live across concurrent tracks.
Time Zones: The live schedule displays in US Central Time (CT) by default, but Zuddl automatically detects and adjusts to the attendee's local device time zone when viewing the schedule in the portal.
Rebroadcasts: Main sessions run live. While there are no separate scheduled regional rebroadcast tracks, on-demand session recordings become available directly inside the portal shortly after each session wraps.

## Speaker Q&A Mechanics
Virtual Attendees: Post questions and upvote peer questions using the Q&A panel on the right side of the screen adjacent to the video livestream.
In-Person Attendees: Submit and upvote questions via the conference mobile app under Sessions > [Select Session] > Q&A.
Moderation: Session chairs/moderators read the highest-voted questions aloud to speakers during the live Q&A block at the end of each talk.

## Discord Community & Technical Support
Accessing Discord: An invitation link is provided in the registration confirmation email and inside the event portal.
Help & Troubleshooting: Dedicated #conf-help / #troubleshooting channels exist in the server. A Discord tips and setup guide is linked in the confirmation email. For persistent access issues, direct users to email conf@posit.co.
Server Availability: Open year-round as an ongoing community hub for Posit users and developers.

## Virtual Swag Bag
Access: A link to the digital swag bag (custom wallpapers, partner perks, software credits, and downloadable goodies) is distributed via email and posted directly in the virtual portal resource center.

## On-Demand Recordings & YouTube Timeline
Event Portal Access: Full video recordings remain accessible on Zuddl for all registered in-person and virtual attendees for 3 months post-conference.
YouTube Release: Talks will be publicly published on the https://www.youtube.com/@PositPBC 3 months after the conference concludes (by January).
Workshops Note: In-person workshops are neither streamed nor recorded. Workshop materials and GitHub repos are posted at https://github.com/posit-conf-2026/ BEFORE the workshops begin so attendees can follow along.

## Accessibility & Live Captioning
Captions: Automated live closed captioning (AI-generated English captions/subtitles) is available across all main stage and track livestreams via the video player controls in Zuddl.

## Ticket Sharing & Group Viewing
Single User Policy: Each virtual registration is assigned to a single unique attendee email and allows one concurrent login session on Zuddl.
Team Viewing: For teams watching together in a shared conference room, a single registered feed can be projected, but individual access to Discord, interactive Q&A, and on-demand portal tracking requires separate individual registrations.

## Virtual Participation in Birds of a Feather (BoF)
Format: Select Birds of a Feather (BoF) networking sessions will offer virtual breakout tables or dedicated Discord voice/text channels. Specific virtual BoF rooms and schedules are published inside the Zuddl portal and Discord server during the conference.
