# posit::conf(2026) Quick Reference: On-Site Extras

## Photography & Lanyard Color-Coding System
Standard Lanyards: Indicate standard consent for event photography, crowd shots, and social media coverage.
Opt-Out Lanyards (Distinct Color): Available at the Registration Desk for any attendee who prefers not to be photographed or recorded. Photographers and videographers are instructed not to capture identifiable photos or close-ups of attendees wearing opt-out lanyards.

## Code of Conduct Reporting Channels & Confidentiality
Reporting Methods: Reports can be submitted in person at the Registration Desk, by calling/texting the dedicated hotline (844-448-1212), or by emailing codeofconduct@posit.co.
Confidentiality vs. Anonymity: Official reporting channels are strictly confidential but not fully anonymous, as response teams need contact details to follow up, investigate details, and ensure immediate physical and psychological safety for all involved.

## Health, Masks & COVID Rapid Tests
Mask Policy: Wearing a mask is optional and welcomed throughout the venue.
On-Site Supplies: High-filtration masks (KN95/N95) and complimentary rapid COVID-19 antigen tests are stocked and freely available upon request at the Registration Desk for anyone feeling unwell or requesting extra precautions.

## Meeting Posit Software Engineers & Package Maintainers
Where to Connect: Posit engineers, core maintainers, and developer advocates will be present throughout the conference in The Lounge, during track Q&A sessions, and attending relevant Birds of a Feather (BoF) discussions.

## Hex Sticker Wall & Book Signings
Hex Wall: Located in The Lounge / Exhibit Hall. Attendees can take hex stickers for their favorite open-source packages and contribute their own community stickers. Beyond the wall, sticker drops happen randomly throughout the conference, mostly in The Lounge.
Author Book Signings: Scheduled open-source author and speaker book signing sessions will take place in The Lounge near the Hex Wall during afternoon breaks and the Monday Welcome Reception.

## Swag
Out of extra swag? Come back to the swag table on Wednesday afternoon — if there are leftovers, attendees can take another. Everyone gets one first.

## Enterprise Product Specialist Consults (Ask an Expert)
1-on-1 Architecture & Deployment Support: Dedicated stations in The Lounge where attendees can book or drop in for 1-on-1 consultations with Posit Enterprise product specialists covering Workbench, Connect, Package Manager, and cloud/hybrid architecture.

## Exhibitor & Sponsor Hall Hours
Location: The Lounge / Exhibit Hall.
Operating Hours: Open daily in alignment with conference schedule hours—specifically during morning check-in, morning/afternoon networking breaks, lunch hours, and the Monday Welcome Reception.

## Community Job Board & Career Networking
Physical Job Board: Located on dedicated display boards in The Lounge for posting open data science, engineering, and ML roles or adding "seeking opportunities" cards.
Digital Extension: Paired with the dedicated #job-board channel in the official Posit Conference Discord server.
