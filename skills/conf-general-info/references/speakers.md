# posit::conf(2026) Quick Reference: Speakers

## AV Equipment & Slide Specifications
Slide Aspect Ratio: Standard 16:9 widescreen.
Stage & Podium Setup: Speaker podiums are equipped with HDMI connections, standard USB-C/DisplayPort adapters, a dedicated confidence monitor, a presentation clicker/slide advancer, and wireless microphones (lavalier or headset).
Backup & Presentation Decks: Speakers are encouraged to test their slides during morning AV check windows before track sessions begin and bring a backup copy of their slides on a USB flash drive.

## Speaker Session-Change Requests
Policy: Session times are locked into the public program to coordinate streaming, closed captioning, room capacity, and attendee schedules.
Emergency Changes: If a speaker experiences unavoidable travel delays, illness, or an emergency, they must notify the Speaker Operations Team immediately via email or in person at the Speaker Check-in / Registration Desk. Slot swaps or remote pre-recorded backups are handled on a case-by-case basis.

## Visa Invitation Letters
How to Request: Registered attendees requiring an official visa support/invitation letter for US B-1/B-2 or ESTA visa applications should email conf@posit.co with their full legal name (as written on passport), organization/institution, passport number, date of birth, and registration confirmation number.

## Visa Delay or Denial Policy
Policy & Refunds: If an attendee’s visa application is delayed or denied and they cannot travel to the US, they are eligible to convert their in-person registration to a virtual ticket and receive a refund for the price difference (or request a full refund if unable to attend virtually), provided they notify conf@posit.co with proof of denial/delay before the event.

## US Travel Best Practices
Documentation: Keep a printed copy of your registration confirmation, hotel reservation, return flight itinerary, and Posit visa invitation letter in carry-on luggage to present to US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) upon arrival.
Entry Requirements: Ensure your passport has at least 6 months of validity beyond your planned stay, and ensure ESTA approval or valid entry visa is secured well before departure.
